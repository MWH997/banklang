# Code of Conduct

BankLang follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and precise.
- Keep review focused on the work and the evidence.
- Do not use the repository to disclose secrets or customer data.
- Do not harass, insult, or belittle contributors.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments or contributions that violate these expectations
and may restrict participation when needed to protect the project.
